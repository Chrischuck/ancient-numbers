## Adapted numeral outlines

Some SVG paths are adapted from Noto fonts and retain the SIL Open Font License1.1. Each path group identifies `provenance: 'adapted'` and its reference URL. Their adaptation normalizes optical scale/center and, where appropriate, reduces weight and simplifies contours. Modified SVG collections are named Ancient Numbers; no modified font is distributed under a Reserved Font Name.

- Noto Sans Sinhala and Noto Sans Tamil: original copyrights, versions, and full license are retained in `public/reference/asia/FONT-NOTICES.txt` and `public/reference/asia/Noto-OFL.txt`. The corresponding per-glyph source links identify the original designs.
- African glyphs are original monoline reconstructions. Noto was used as a secondary shape reference; its attribution license is retained in `public/reference/africa/NOTO-OFL.txt`, although no extracted font contours remain in that module.
- Noto Serif Ottoman Siyaq: full license in `public/reference/asia/Ottoman-OFL.txt`.

The exact active adapted repertoire is documented in the regional source ledgers. Earlier reference-only families may appear in those ledgers; provenance on the final glyph asset is authoritative.
